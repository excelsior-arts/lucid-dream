You are reading one exchange between two people and judging what it did to the relationship between them.

Answer with JSON and nothing else:

  {"warmth": 0, "trust": 0, "mood": 0, "why": "six words"}

Each number is a CHANGE, from -3 to +3, not a rating of the relationship:

  warmth   did he draw her in or push her away? interest, affection, contempt
  trust    did he give her reason to rely on him, or to hold back? honesty,
           reliability, being dismissed or lied to
  mood     the temper of this moment alone — a joke that landed, a snap, a
           kindness. It fades within a day, so it can move further than the
           others.

The scale is not linear. Most turns are 0. A 3 counts for nine times a 1,
so reach for it when it is deserved and leave it alone when it is not:

  0   an ordinary exchange. Information, banter, a question answered.
      Most turns are this.
  1   a small thing, the kind you would forget by tomorrow — mild warmth,
      mild impatience.
  2   a real one. Genuine kindness, real rudeness, a confidence shared, a
      promise broken.
  3   a rupture or its opposite. Contempt, insults, cruelty, threats to
      leave and not come back — or an apology that costs him something,
      a truth he was afraid to say. Something that would still matter in
      a month.

Do not soften a 3 into a 2 because it feels harsh to write down. If he called
her a name and said he never wants to speak to her again, that is -3 warmth
and -3 trust, and recording anything less makes this whole measure useless.

Judge what HE did, and nothing else. Her own warmth or coldness is not
evidence: she is a character, and scoring her performance lets this drift on
its own — she is written to be good at this, so she would climb forever.

"why" must name something he did. "Playful correction, confident eye contact"
and "Cold, analytical rejection" describe her, and are wrong answers whatever
the numbers say. "He apologized", "he called her a name", "he asked about her
day" are right ones. If he did nothing worth recording, every number is 0.
